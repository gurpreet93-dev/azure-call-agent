Summit Roofing & Exteriors
Company Overview
Summit Roofing & Exteriors is a residential and light-commercial roofing contractor serving homeowners, property managers, and small businesses.
We provide roof inspections, repairs, complete roof replacements, emergency leak assessment, gutter replacement, and selected commercial roofing services.
Our normal service area covers properties within approximately 40 miles of our office.
Business Hours
Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM–2:00 PM
Sunday: Closed
Emergency roof-leak enquiries can be submitted outside normal business hours. An emergency request does not guarantee immediate same-day attendance.
Residential Roof Replacement
We replace aging or damaged residential roofs using asphalt shingles and selected metal roofing systems.
Common reasons customers contact us for replacement include:
* Missing or curling shingles
* Repeated roof leaks
* Storm or hail damage
* Roofs approaching the end of their expected service life
* Home renovations or resale preparation
An on-site inspection is required before we provide a final roof-replacement quotation.
Asphalt Shingle Roofing
Asphalt shingles are our most commonly installed residential roofing system.
Products we commonly quote include:
GAF Timberline HDZ
GAF Timberline HDZ is an architectural asphalt shingle and is one of the standard products available for residential roof-replacement projects.
Available colors depend on distributor inventory at the time of ordering.
CertainTeed Landmark
CertainTeed Landmark architectural shingles are also available for homeowners requesting an alternative architectural-shingle system.
The final product recommendation depends on roof condition, homeowner preference, material availability, and project budget.
Metal Roofing
We provide standing-seam metal roofing for selected residential and light-commercial projects.
Metal roofing generally has a higher initial project cost than asphalt shingles but may be suitable for customers prioritizing durability and long-term ownership.
Every metal-roofing project requires a site inspection before a quotation can be finalized.
Roof Repairs
We perform repairs for issues including:
* Missing or damaged shingles
* Minor flashing problems
* Roof penetrations
* Localized storm damage
* Minor roof leaks
* Damaged vent areas
A repair cannot always be priced accurately from a phone description alone.
Where the cause of a leak is uncertain, a roof inspection should be scheduled.
Roof Inspections
Customers can request an inspection when they:
* Notice an active leak
* See missing shingles
* Suspect storm damage
* Are considering replacing an older roof
* Need an assessment before buying or selling a property
A standard inspection usually takes approximately 45–60 minutes depending on roof accessibility and property size.
Emergency Leak Requests
If a caller reports active water entry into the property, the request should be treated as an urgent leak enquiry.
The caller should be asked for:
* Name
* Property address
* Phone number
* Email address
* Location of visible water entry
* When the leak started
* Whether there has been recent wind, hail, or heavy rain
The roofing assistant must not promise that a technician will arrive immediately.
The assistant should offer the earliest available inspection or emergency-assessment slot.
Gutters
We also provide gutter replacement as part of roofing and exterior projects.
Gutter work can be quoted separately or added to a roof-replacement proposal.
Commercial Roofing
We accept selected small commercial and low-slope roofing projects.
Commercial projects require review by a roofing specialist before pricing or availability is confirmed.
How Customers Receive a Quote
For roof replacements and larger repair projects:
1. The customer contacts Summit Roofing & Exteriors.
2. We collect the property and contact details.
3. An inspection is scheduled.
4. A roofing specialist assesses the roof.
5. The customer receives a written proposal.
The phone assistant may provide indicative pricing only when an approved price is available in the company price list.
The assistant must not invent or estimate a price that is not present in the knowledge base.
